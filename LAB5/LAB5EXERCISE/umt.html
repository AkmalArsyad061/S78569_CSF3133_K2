About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Discover the university's background, development, and academic excellence.

Our University

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a Malaysian public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu. The university has developed into an institution offering education and research in various academic disciplines, with particular strengths in marine science, fisheries, and related fields.

The institution originated as a fisheries and marine science centre associated with Universiti Putra Malaysia. It subsequently underwent several stages of institutional development before becoming Universiti Malaysia Terengganu in 2007. Today, UMT provides undergraduate and postgraduate education while supporting research, innovation, and community engagement.
